About Talking Tipps

Our Story

Talking Tipps Speech Therapy was founded by Gifty Ayoka, a passionate speech and language therapist with a vision to make quality speech therapy accessible to everyone in Africa. Starting in Ghana, we've helped hundreds of children and adults overcome communication challenges. Now, we're expanding across Africa through secure online teletherapy.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, evidence-based speech and language therapy to individuals across Africa, empowering them to communicate confidently and achieve their full potential.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading provider of speech and language therapy services, breaking down barriers to communication and making expert care available to every community.

Our Services

Clear support, explained in plain language

We avoid clinical jargon wherever we can — here's what we help with, described the way we'd explain it to a worried parent or concerned adult.

Speech Delay

For children who are late to start talking or have difficulty pronouncing words. Our therapists work with your child to develop clearer speech and build confidence.

Language Delay

For children who understand language but struggle to express themselves. We help children build vocabulary, form sentences, and communicate effectively.

Autism Spectrum Disorder

Supporting children with autism to develop communication skills, understand social cues, and express their needs, using evidence-based, child-friendly approaches.

Down Syndrome

Helping children with Down syndrome develop speech and language skills through tailored, engaging therapy sessions that celebrate their unique abilities.

Cerebral Palsy

Working with children who have cerebral palsy to improve their ability to communicate using speech, AAC devices, or other methods.

Stammering / Stuttering

Building confidence in children who stutter, using proven techniques to help them speak more smoothly and without fear.

Speech Sound Disorders

For children who struggle to produce certain sounds correctly. We help them learn proper articulation through fun, engaging exercises.

AAC (Alternative Communication)

Supporting children who need alternative ways to communicate — using devices, pictures, and other tools to help them express themselves.

Early Intervention

Early support for babies and toddlers at risk of speech or language delays. The earlier we start, the better the outcomes.

Parent Coaching

Practical strategies to encourage communication at home — because therapy continues between sessions.

Is this your child?

Aphasia (Stroke Rehabilitation)

For adults who have lost language ability after a stroke. We help rebuild speaking, understanding, reading, and writing skills.

Dysarthria

For adults with weak or unclear speech. We work on strengthening speech muscles and improving clarity.

Voice Therapy

For adults with voice problems — whether from overuse, injury, or medical conditions. We help restore a healthy, strong voice.

Cognitive Communication Therapy

For adults with cognitive challenges affecting communication. We work on memory, attention, problem-solving, and social communication.

Is this you or a loved one?

School Consultations

Working with schools to support students with communication needs. We assess, train staff, and create communication-friendly environments.

Teacher Training

Equipping teachers with practical strategies to support students with speech, language, and communication difficulties in the classroom.

Corporate Communication Training

Helping professionals communicate more effectively — public speaking, clarity, and interpersonal skills.

Workshops

Group workshops on communication, disability inclusion, and supporting individuals with speech and language challenges.

Disability Inclusion Consulting

Advising organisations on creating inclusive environments for people with communication difficulties.

Teletherapy

Real therapy, wherever you are

Teletherapy brings expert speech and language therapy directly to you — wherever you are in Africa. Using secure video sessions, our qualified therapists provide the same high-quality care as in-person sessions, without the need to travel.

  • Access from anywhere in Africa
  • No travel time or costs
  • Comfort of home, flexible scheduling
  • Continuity of care, same quality as in-person
Ghana Kenya + across Africa

What you need

  • Computer, tablet, or smartphone with a camera
  • Good internet connection (10 Mbps download / 1 Mbps upload recommended)
  • A quiet, private space
  • Webcam and microphone — a headset is recommended

Is it effective?

Yes. Research shows that teletherapy is just as effective as in-person therapy for many speech and language conditions. Our therapists use interactive tools, engaging activities, and proven techniques to deliver excellent results.

Privacy & confidentiality

Your privacy is our priority. All sessions are conducted on a secure, encrypted platform, separate from this website. We comply with professional privacy standards to protect your personal and health information.

12 months

18 months
